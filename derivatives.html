THE NOTATION

One idea.
Different lenses.

dy/dxthe derivative

The instantaneous rate of change: the slope of the tangent line when the gap becomes infinitesimally small.

Δy/Δxthe secant slope

Average change over a finite interval. This is what the blue line measures.

∂y/∂xa partial derivative

The change in one variable while holding the other variables constant.

δy/δxcontext-dependent

Often used for a finite variation or perturbation. It is not a universal replacement for dy/dx.

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR AI

Training follows the slope downhill.

A model changes its parameters in the direction that reduces the loss. Derivatives tell it which small changes help—and by how much.
